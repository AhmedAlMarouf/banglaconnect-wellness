# BanglaConnect Wellness

**BanglaConnect Wellness** is a community-driven nonprofit initiative dedicated to connecting Bangladeshi individuals and families with reliable health information, wellness resources, and healthcare navigation support in Canada.

Our goal is simple: **to make health information easier to understand, healthcare resources easier to navigate, and healthy living more accessible to our community.**

We recognize that language, cultural differences, limited familiarity with the healthcare system, and misinformation can create barriers to accessing appropriate health information and services. BanglaConnect Wellness aims to help bridge these gaps through culturally relevant health education, bilingual resources in **Bangla and English**, community awareness programs, and innovative digital tools.

Starting with the Bangladeshi community in **Alberta, particularly Edmonton and Calgary**, we aim to promote preventive health, improve health literacy, support healthcare navigation, and encourage healthier and more informed communities.

As we grow, we envision incorporating responsible **AI-powered tools** that can help community members find trusted health resources, understand the Canadian healthcare system, prepare for healthcare visits, and access reliable information in a more convenient and understandable way.

BanglaConnect Wellness is built around the belief that **better connections lead to better health**—connections to knowledge, healthcare resources, professionals, and one another.

**Connecting Community. Empowering Health. Enriching Lives.**
